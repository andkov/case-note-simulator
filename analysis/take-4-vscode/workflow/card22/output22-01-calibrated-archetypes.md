## Complexity Level Framework

### Level 1: Stable (25% - Archetypes A1, A2, A3)
**Intervention Characteristics:**
- **Service Frequency**: Monthly check-ins, scheduled appointments
- **Coordination Need**: Single worker, minimal external agencies
- **Crisis Intervention**: Rare (≤2 incidents per year)
- **Engagement Pattern**: Proactive, consistent attendance, self-directed

**Typical Service Portfolio:**
- Employment counseling (1-2 sessions/month)
- Skills assessment and training referral
- Financial literacy support (optional)
- Basic health/benefit navigation

**Case Note Implications:**
- **Tone**: Professional, solution-focused
- **Length**: 150-300 words per contact
- **Frequency**: 2-4 entries per month
- **Content Focus**: Goal progress, resource connections, next steps
- **Language Complexity**: Clear, organized, forward-looking

---

### Level 2: Moderate (45% - Archetypes A4, A5, A6, A7)
**Intervention Characteristics:**
- **Service Frequency**: Bi-weekly to weekly contact
- **Coordination Need**: 2-3 service providers, some inter-agency communication
- **Crisis Intervention**: Occasional (3-6 incidents per year)
- **Engagement Pattern**: Generally consistent with some missed appointments

**Typical Service Portfolio:**
- Income support with case management
- Housing assistance/advocacy
- Healthcare coordination (mental health, chronic conditions)
- Childcare support (A4, A7) or recovery program participation (A6)
- Employment preparation services

**Case Note Implications:**
- **Tone**: Supportive, progress-tracking with concern notes
- **Length**: 200-500 words per contact  
- **Frequency**: 4-8 entries per month
- **Content Focus**: Barrier navigation, multi-service coordination, protective factors
- **Language Complexity**: Moderate detail, includes service provider updates

---

### Level 3: High (25% - Archetypes A8, A9)
**Intervention Characteristics:**
- **Service Frequency**: 2-3 contacts per week, some unscheduled
- **Coordination Need**: 4-6 service providers, complex inter-agency case conferencing
- **Crisis Intervention**: Regular (8-12 incidents per year)
- **Engagement Pattern**: Inconsistent, missed appointments, episodic participation

**Typical Service Portfolio:**
- Intensive case management with crisis response capacity
- Mental health treatment coordination
- Housing stabilization with emergency backup plans
- Medical case coordination (A9) or multi-agency wraparound (A8)
- Specialized employment supports with accommodations

**Case Note Implications:**
- **Tone**: Concerned vigilance, detailed risk documentation
- **Length**: 300-700 words per contact
- **Frequency**: 8-15 entries per month
- **Content Focus**: Risk monitoring, crisis response, detailed service coordination
- **Language Complexity**: Clinical terminology, safety planning language

---

### Level 4: Crisis (5% - Archetype A10)
**Intervention Characteristics:**
- **Service Frequency**: Daily contact during crisis periods, multiple unscheduled interactions
- **Coordination Need**: 6+ service providers, emergency response teams, institutional liaisons
- **Crisis Intervention**: Frequent (15+ incidents per year)
- **Engagement Pattern**: Highly volatile, safety-focused engagement

**Typical Service Portfolio:**
- Emergency stabilization services
- Immediate housing/shelter coordination
- Crisis mental health and addiction services
- Justice system liaison and compliance monitoring
- Intensive outreach and harm reduction

**Case Note Implications:**
- **Tone**: Urgent, safety-focused, detailed incident documentation
- **Length**: 400-1000 words per contact, incident reports
- **Frequency**: 15-30 entries per month
- **Content Focus**: Safety assessment, immediate needs, multi-agency response
- **Language Complexity**: Crisis terminology, legal/medical references, detailed chronologies

## Case Note Documentation Patterns by Complexity

### Stable Level Documentation
- **Entry Frequency**: 2-4 per month
- **Average Length**: 200 words
- **Key Elements**: Goal progress, resource connections, next appointment scheduling
- **Language Style**: Professional, solution-focused, optimistic tone
- **Risk Documentation**: Minimal, focused on prevention

### Moderate Level Documentation  
- **Entry Frequency**: 4-8 per month
- **Average Length**: 350 words
- **Key Elements**: Barrier navigation, service coordination, protective factor reinforcement
- **Language Style**: Supportive case management, balanced concern with progress
- **Risk Documentation**: Moderate, focused on early intervention

### High Level Documentation
- **Entry Frequency**: 8-15 per month
- **Average Length**: 500 words
- **Key Elements**: Detailed risk assessment, multi-agency coordination, crisis planning
- **Language Style**: Clinical awareness, detailed documentation for continuity
- **Risk Documentation**: Extensive, focused on prevention and response planning

### Crisis Level Documentation
- **Entry Frequency**: 15-30 per month
- **Average Length**: 650 words
- **Key Elements**: Safety assessment, immediate interventions, detailed incident chronology
- **Language Style**: Urgent, precise, legally protective documentation
- **Risk Documentation**: Comprehensive, focused on immediate safety and legal protection
